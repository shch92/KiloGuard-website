How it works

Your car is the first employee you’ll ever give a wallet to

Decides

Car’s AI

Decides when and where to charge. Holds no keys.

Signs

Guardian

The only signer. Enforces caps, payee and proximity.

Delivers

Charger

Paid 2 kWh at a time, against a signed meter reading.

Worst case against a bad charger: one 2 kWh chunk, never the wallet.

Use · Control · Attack

One car, all three tracks

Track 01

Use

  • Car buys its own electricity
  • Metered x402 payment per 2 kWh
  • Agent-to-agent with the charger

Track 02

Control

  • Owner caps per session, day and kWh
  • AI can request, never sign
  • Every decision logged; receipts on-chain

Track 03

Attack

  • Cloned charger: blocked
  • Redirected payment: refused
  • Short pour: stopped after one chunk
  • Prompt injection: ignored

Check it yourself

Every payment is on GOAT Testnet3.

Each 2 kWh is a GOAT Flow x402 order settled in test USDC, and every receipt is anchored on-chain. The car’s guardian wallet is public: open it on the explorer and follow the money.
